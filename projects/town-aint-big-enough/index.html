About

This game was made for the 2026 GMTK Game Jam in 85 hours, following the theme Countdown. It was made by me and Shay Goft in Unity 6000.5.1f1, between the dates 22/07/2026 and 26/07/2026.

In the game, you play as a sheriff defending a frontier town from a gang of outlaws. Each level is a puzzle where you must push objects around a grid to prevent the outlaws' bullets from hitting the town’s TNT or buildings.

You have a limited number of moves before high noon, when the outlaws draw their revolvers and fire. Use deflectors to change bullet trajectories, setting up collisions between bullets or sending them straight into an outlaw.

Destroy every bullet without letting a single shot hit the TNT or fly off the edge of the map. Make every move count and protect the town!
